## Design Context

### Users
Hackathon judges need to understand the product and see proof of a self-directing loop in seconds. The ongoing user is an agency operator who needs to inspect a lead, intervene when safety gates stop the system, and open deeper worker diagnostics only when necessary.

### Brand Personality
Decisive, auditable, and warm. Callan should feel like a capable operating company with visible judgment—not a generic AI dashboard or a novelty agent demo.

### Aesthetic Direction
A focused, dark editorial control room with warm cherry, parchment, and apricot accents. The primary experience is linear and evidence-led; dense tools use progressive disclosure. Avoid generic metric-card grids, neon AI styling, decorative glass effects, and unexplained technical labels.

### Design Principles
1. Explain the autonomous loop before exposing its machinery.
2. Make observed failure, correction, and verification visible as evidence.
3. Keep one primary action per state; move diagnostics into the workbench.
4. Preserve full capability on mobile with a single-column, touch-first flow.
5. Treat mock mode as an honest demonstration state, never as production readiness.
